Lina Seismic Playground is a lightweight experimentation space for testing seismic data workflows, ideas, and tooling before they harden into larger research software.

## What it is for

- Rapid prototyping of seismic workflows
- Testing small utilities and data-processing ideas
- Exploring model and preprocessing variations before formalizing them
